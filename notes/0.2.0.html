What's new

Holoholo for macOS · Version 0.2.0 beta · November 21, 2026

🎉 Holoholo is now in open beta! Thank you to everyone who tried the alpha, reported bugs and shared ideas: this release is a big step for the browser. Enjoy the walk, and keep the feedback coming.

✨ New

  • Peek. Shift-click a link to open it in a card over the page. Press ⌘O to keep it as a tab, send it to another Space, or open it in Split View; ⌘W closes it. Each tab keeps its own Peek. You can also send links to other sites from pinned tabs to Peek (Settings → General).
  • Choose where links from other apps open. Pick a Space for a site, or open such links in a small Peek window (Settings → General → Links from other apps).
  • Space themes. Give each Space, Private Space included, one of 11 watercolors: use the button in the corner of an empty tab, or Theme… in the Space menu.
  • Auto-archive. Tabs you haven't visited for a day, three days or a week move to the Archive. Off by default (Settings → General).
  • Picture in Picture follows you. A playing video moves to a small window when you leave its tab and comes back when you return (Settings → General → Video).
  • Clean links. Copied links lose tracking parameters such as utm_ and fbclid.
  • Export and import Spaces. File → Export Spaces… saves your Spaces, pinned tabs, favorites and Tweaks to a file. Import Spaces… adds them on another Mac and deletes nothing.
  • Never Translate This Site in the translate button's menu; the list is in Settings → General.

💫 Improved

  • Closing a tab takes you back to the tab you were on before.
  • Closing a tab stops its video and sound, Picture in Picture included.
  • Private Space has a new ghost icon 👻, and its lock screen no longer shows the page you left.
  • A new look for the installer window.

🛠 Fixed

  • Links from other apps are no longer lost while the Settings window is open.
  • The sidebar no longer flickers when you hide or show it with ⌘S.
  • The command bar keeps what you typed when Tab switches between search and Actions.

⚠️ Known issue

  • The Back to tab button in Picture in Picture doesn't work yet. Switch to the tab yourself.
