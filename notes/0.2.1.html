What's new

Holoholo for macOS · Version 0.2.1 beta · November 21, 2026

🏡 Holoholo has a home: holoholo.dev.

💫 Improved

  • Links from other apps now open in a small Peek window by default. If you chose Current Space, nothing changes (Settings → General → Links from other apps).
  • Holoholo checks for updates and downloads them in the background on its own, without asking first.
  • Pull to reload: the trackpad clicks along the pull, faster as you pull further.
  • If you deny an app access in Companions, Holoholo remembers and doesn't ask again. A connected app no longer opens Holoholo on its own.
  • The Holoholo name on the welcome screen and in the installer now matches the website.

⚠️ Known issue

  • The Back to tab button in Picture in Picture doesn't work yet. Switch to the tab yourself.
